Who we are

Nutag is operated by CIA SOLUTION LLC ("Nutag", "we"). Contact: info@cia.mn.

02

What Nutag is

Nutag is a collecting game played at real places. On a day you order at a partner café, shop or gym (an "Ortoo"), you get that Ortoo's part of the day, one of the eight parts of a Ger. One of each part raises a Ger at an Ortoo you choose, and it stands for as long as you keep going back there.

03

Your account

  • You sign in with your phone number and a one-time code, verified by our sign-in provider CIA, in the app or on a table's ordering page.
  • One account per person. Using several accounts to get around the game's limits breaks §8.
  • Keep your phone and the codes sent to it safe. You are responsible for what is done on your account.
  • A name must not be abusive, impersonate someone, or be obscene or unlawful. We may change such a name without notice.

04

Game rules

As they stand today:

  • A new player starts with one of each part.
  • Each Ortoo gives you one part a day, its part of the day, however much you order.
  • A part is credited only once the visit is proven: the payment settles, on the gateway's confirmation, or staff approve your order at the counter. Never on the app's word alone.
  • One of each part raises a Ger, one a day at each Ortoo.
  • Each Ortoo sets how long a Ger stands without an approved order of its owner's there: at least 7 days, longer, or never falling. The app warns you two days before. If a Ger falls, 7 parts come back and the Uni is lost.
  • Up to three drops a day can be bought, and they are optional (§6.2).

These numbers are game balance and we may change them. Significant changes are announced in the app beforehand.

06

Payments

6.1. Orders at an Ortoo

  • The seller is the Ortoo, not Nutag. The contract for what you order is between you and that Ortoo.
  • You pay either in the app, to the Ortoo's own Bonum/QPay or Stripe account, or at the counter by cash, card or transfer, which staff record. Nutag takes no cut of what you pay.
  • Questions about quality, missing items or refunds go to the Ortoo first. We'll help with the order record.
  • Prices, taxes and offers are set by the Ortoo. The menu is written by the Ortoo, and we don't warrant that it is accurate.
  • A payment started in the app is cancelled if unpaid after 15 minutes. If the money moves later, your part is still credited: check "Orders" in the app.

6.2. Drops

  • A drop is a consumable: one part, shown on the map before you pay. It is a way to skip a day's walk, and the game is complete without it.
  • A player with a Mongolian (+976) number pays Nutag through Bonum/QPay. Anyone else buys through the App Store or Google Play, where Apple or Google is the seller and their terms apply.
  • The part is credited only once the payment settles. If you paid and no part arrived, write to info@cia.mn.
  • A collected drop is not refundable, except where law or the store's policy requires. Refunds of store purchases are decided by Apple or Google, and we can't override them.

07

Stories

  • After an order of yours is approved at an Ortoo, you can post a story there: a rating, words, and photos or a video. It is public (Privacy Policy §5).
  • Post only what is yours to share and true to your visit. Nothing unlawful, abusive or hateful, nothing that shows someone who didn't agree to it, and no one else's private details.
  • What you post stays yours. You give us a free, non-exclusive licence to show it in the app, on nutag.fun and in the picture the app makes when you share it, for as long as it is up.
  • You can take a story down at any time. We may remove one that breaks these terms. The Ortoo can't edit, hide or remove it.

08

Fair play

The following are not allowed:

  • Faking your location (GPS spoofing), emulators, automated scripts or modified clients;
  • Creating several accounts to get around the daily limits;
  • Recording orders that didn't happen, with or without the help of staff;
  • Knowingly exploiting a bug or loophole;
  • Flooding the API, disrupting the service, or getting into someone else's account;
  • Harassing or threatening other players or an Ortoo's staff.

Where we find a breach we may take back parts and Gers, remove stories, suspend the account, or close it.

09

Real-world safety

  • Follow the traffic rules and stay aware of your surroundings.
  • Don't go onto private property, or try an Ortoo outside its opening hours.
  • A place on the map is not permission to enter it.

You are responsible for your own safety while using the app in the real world.

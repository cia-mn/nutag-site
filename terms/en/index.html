2. What Nutag is

Nutag is a location-based collecting game. When you order at a partner shop or café (an "Ortoo") you collect one of the eight parts of a Ger. Complete all eight and you build a Ger at that venue. You keep it by coming back.

3. Your account

4. Game rules

As they stand today:

  • Each Ortoo credits parts once per day.
  • Parts are credited only after a payment settles — on the gateway's confirmation, never on the app's word.
  • A product yields its parts once, however many you buy. Quantity raises the bill, not the yield.
  • Eight parts build a Ger; one build per Ortoo per day.
  • If you do not order at that Ortoo for 7 days, your Ger there falls: 7 parts return and the uni is destroyed.

These numbers are game balance and we may change them. Significant changes are announced in the app beforehand.

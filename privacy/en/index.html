Privacy Policy

This policy covers the Nutag mobile app and the services under the nutag.mn domain. It states what we collect, why, who receives it, how long we keep it, and what you can ask us to do.

1. Who is responsible

Your personal data is controlled and processed by [Legal entity name] ("we", "Nutag"). Contact: privacy@nutag.mn.

2. In short

  • Accounts are phone-number only — no password, no email address.
  • Location is requested only when you tap "find me" on the map or look at drops. There is no background location tracking of any kind.
  • The camera is used only to scan a QR code. Frames never leave your device.
  • The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDK.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • You can request deletion of your account from inside the app (§8).

3. What we collect

DataWhenWhy
Phone number At sign-in. A one-time code (OTP) is sent by SMS. To sign you in and keep one account per person. The number is received and verified by our identity provider CIA (api.cia.mn).
Account identifier (sub) At account creation. The stable id your game data hangs off. It is also sent to RevenueCat as app_user_id so purchases follow the account rather than the handset. Your default avatar is drawn from this id.
Location (GPS) Only when you tap "find me" on the map or view drops. Never at launch. To sort nearby Ortoos and to place the day's drops around you.

Player handle When you set it in your profile. Optional. The name you are known by. The game is fully playable without one.
Game data When you order at an Ortoo or build a Ger. Parts collected, Gers built, which venue you visited on which day. This is what the daily caps and the 7-day activity clock are computed from.
Purchases When you pay for an order at a venue, or buy in-app. Amount, status, which vendor, which products, time. Nutag never receives card numbers — payment is handled by Bonum/QPay or Stripe.
Vendor promo interactions When a vendor's promo is shown or tapped on an Ortoo page. To report to that vendor how their own banner performed. This is a first-party counter, not a third-party ad network.
Technical logs On each request to our server. IP address, timestamp and request type, kept briefly in server logs for security, abuse prevention and debugging.

More detail on location

There are two uses, both only with your permission:

  • Nearby Ortoos. Your coordinates are used to sort the list and are not stored.
  • Drops. The server computes points offset from your position by a random bearing and 150–500 metres, and stores those offset points as the day's drop locations against your account. The point recorded is deliberately not where you were.

The app has no background-location mode at all. You can revoke the permission in your device settings at any time; the map still works, but nearby sorting and drops stop.

4. What we do not do

  • No third-party advertising SDK (no AdMob, Facebook, AppsFlyer).
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDK (no Firebase, Crashlytics, Sentry, Mixpanel, Amplitude).
  • No tracking of your activity in other companies' apps or websites.
  • We never sell, rent, or share personal data for advertising.
  • We never give your phone number to vendors.

8. Your rights

  • Access and correction. Change your handle in the profile at any time. Ask for a copy of the rest at privacy@nutag.mn.
  • Deletion. App → Profile → Delete account. The request goes to CIA, which notifies every service tied to the account (Nutag included) and coordinates the deletion. It completes within 30 days and is irreversible: parts, Gers and history do not come back.
  • Withdraw permissions. Revoke location or camera access in your device settings at any time.
  • Complaints. Write to us first. If unresolved, you may complain to the Mongolian authority responsible for personal data protection.

10. Security

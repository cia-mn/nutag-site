Privacy Policy

What we keep about you, and why.

This policy covers the Nutag app for iPhone and Android and the services under nutag.fun. It says what we collect, why, who receives it, how long we keep it, and what you can ask of us.

01

Who is responsible

Your personal data is controlled and processed by CIA SOLUTION LLC ("we", "Nutag"). Contact: info@cia.mn.

02

In short

  • You sign in with a phone number only. No password, no email address.
  • Location is asked for when the map opens. Say no and the map and the list still work. Nothing tracks you in the background.
  • The camera reads QR codes on your phone. Photos and videos leave it only when you post them in a story.
  • A story you post is public: anyone can read it, with your name.
  • The app has no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDK.
  • We don't sell your data, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • You can ask for your account to be deleted from inside the app (§10).

04

Location, use by use.

Each use works only with your permission:

  • Around here. Your position sorts the list of Ortoos and is not stored.
  • Drops. The server picks points a random direction and 150–500 metres away from you and stores those offset points as the day's drops on your account. The point stored is deliberately not where you were.
  • Directions. Your position and where you are going are sent from our server to Google Routes, to draw the way. Not stored.
  • Place search. What you type, with a point near you, is sent from our server to Google Places. The answer is kept for a day so the same search isn't paid for twice, and it is not tied to you.
  • Weather. Your position rounded to 0.1° (about 10 km) is sent from our server to SkyData, through Hamuga.

For directions, place search and weather, our server makes the request, so Google and SkyData never see your phone's IP address. The app has no background-location mode at all. You can withdraw the permission in your phone's settings at any time: the map still works, but sorting by distance, drops and directions from where you stand stop.

05

Stories are public.

You can post a story at an Ortoo after an order of yours there has been approved: a rating, up to 1000 characters, and up to five photos or one video. Anyone can see it, in the app and through our API, with your name and your Nuudelchin. Your phone number is never shown.

There is one story per player per Ortoo: posting again there replaces it. You can take a story down at any time, and its photos and video are deleted with it. The Ortoo cannot edit, hide or delete your story.

If you share a story out, the app makes a picture of it and hands it to your phone's share sheet. Where it goes from there is up to you and the app you pick.

06

What we don't do

  • No third-party advertising SDK (no AdMob, Facebook, AppsFlyer). The ads on an Ortoo's page are placed by us, and the advertiser is named on each one.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDK (no Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Sentry, Mixpanel, Amplitude). The one Firebase part of the app is Cloud Messaging, which only delivers notifications.
  • No marketing notifications. A notification is always about something of yours.
  • No tracking of what you do in other companies' apps or websites.
  • We never sell or rent personal data, or share it for advertising.
  • We never give your phone number to an Ortoo or an advertiser.
  • The typical-traffic times on the map come from our own timed drives along a few roads, not from players' phones.

08

How long we keep it

  • Account, name, game data: until the account is deleted.
  • Stories: until you take one down, post a new one at the same Ortoo, or delete your account. The photos and video go with it.
  • Order and payment records: 1 year, or longer where Mongolian accounting or tax law requires it, detached from the account once it is deleted.
  • Drop points: only needed for that day's play; removed with the account.
  • Notification token: until you sign out on that phone.
  • A business's application: while we work with that business. Ask us at info@cia.mn and we delete it.
  • Server logs: 30 days.
  • Deletion requests: completed within 30 days (§10).

09

Where it is stored

Nutag's servers and database are in Singapore, Mongolia and Hong Kong. Images, photos and videos are stored in Cloudflare R2. Partners such as CIA, RevenueCat, Stripe, Apple, Google and Cloudflare may process parts of your data on their own infrastructure outside Mongolia.

10

Your rights

  • See and correct. Change your name in your profile at any time. Ask for a copy of the rest at info@cia.mn.
  • Delete a story. On the Ortoo's page, take it down. Its photos and video are deleted with it.
  • Delete your account. App → Profile → Delete account. The request goes to CIA, our sign-in provider, and we delete your Nutag data with it within 30 days. It can't be undone: parts, Gers, stories and history don't come back.
  • Withdraw a permission. Location, camera, photos, microphone and notifications can each be turned off in your phone's settings at any time.
  • Complain. Write to us first. If that doesn't settle it, you may complain to the Mongolian authority responsible for personal data protection.

11

Children

Nutag is not meant for anyone under 16, and we don't knowingly collect their data; if we learn we have, we delete it. If you are under 18, buy only with a parent's or guardian's consent.

12

Security

  • Every connection uses HTTPS/TLS.
  • In the app, sign-in tokens are kept in the phone's secure store (iOS Keychain, Android Keystore).
  • An Ortoo's payment credentials are encrypted at rest with AES-GCM.
  • We store no passwords: signing in is by one-time code.

13

Changes

If this policy changes, we publish the new version here with a new date, and tell you about significant changes in the app.

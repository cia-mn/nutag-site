Nutag is a collecting game played at real places in Ulaanbaatar. Each partner café and shop welcomes you with one part of a Ger on the days you order there.

How it works

Three small steps, on your usual way around town.

  1. 1.0 — Ortoo

    An Ortoo around here

    Every partner café, shop and gym on the map and in the “Around here” list is an Ortoo. The name comes from the relay stations of the Mongol Empire, where messengers rested and changed horses.

  2. 2.0 — Order

    Today's part

    Each Ortoo gives one part a day and shows you which one before you order. Once you pay in the app, or staff approve your order at the counter, that part is yours.

  3. 3.0 — Ger

    Raise a Ger

    With all eight parts, tap “Raise a Ger” and it goes up at an Ortoo where you order. Each Ger stands at one Ortoo, and you can hold as many as you like.

Parts of a Ger

Eight parts, one warm Ger.

Each Ortoo gives the eight parts in turn, one a day. Eight days in a row at one place make a full set, and a few different Ortoos get you there sooner.

  1. Toono

    The round window at the crown. Smoke goes out, sunlight comes in.

  2. Bagana

    The two posts that hold up the toono. By custom, nobody walks between them.

  3. Khaalga

    The wooden door that welcomes guests, always facing south.

  4. Orkh

    The felt flap over the toono, opened each morning and drawn closed at night.

  5. Esgii

    Sheep's-wool felt over the roof and walls that keeps the Ger warm.

  6. Busluur

    Three ropes around the outside that hold the Ger snug.

  7. Khana

    The folding lattice walls. A Ger's size is counted in khana.

  8. Uni

    Long roof poles from khana to toono. The only part lost if a Ger falls.

Questions

Good to know

Do I start from zero?

No. Every new player starts with all eight parts, so your first Ger can go up at the first Ortoo where your order is approved.

What counts as a visit?

Your first approved order of the day at an Ortoo. An order is approved when you pay in the app, or when staff confirm it at the counter.

Do I get more parts if I order more?

No. One coffee or ten, each Ortoo gives one part a day, so just order what you'd like.

What if I can't go back for a while?

Your Ger keeps standing while you keep visiting the Ortoo where you raised it. After a long gap it can fall, but each place sets how long: at least 7 days, longer at some, and at some it never falls. Even then you keep 7 of the 8 parts, so you're only one Uni short of raising it again.

Can someone knock my Ger down?

No. Nobody can touch another player's Ger. There are no points and no leaderboard: the Gers you have standing are your score.

Is the part I get from an order random?

No. Every Ortoo shows today's part and tomorrow's before you order, so you always know what you'll get.

02 / The parts of a Ger

Put together
like a real one.

A Mongolian Ger is made of eight main parts. Every Ortoo hands them out one a day, in the order a real Ger goes up, so eight visits to any one Ortoo is a whole Ger.

About every part

Toono

The round crown at the top. Every roof pole rests on it.

Bagana

Two upright posts that hold the crown up from below.

Khaalga

The wooden door, always facing south.

Orkh

The square felt flap that covers the crown.

Esgii

The sheep's-wool felt cover that keeps the Ger warm.

Busluur

The ropes drawn around the outside to hold it tight.

Khana

The folding lattice wall that makes the Ger's circle.

Uni

The long thin roof poles. The only part lost when a Ger falls.

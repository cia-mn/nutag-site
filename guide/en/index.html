01 — Nutag

What is Nutag? It turns an ordinary day into a game.

Nutag is a collecting game played at real places in Ulaanbaatar. Every partner café, shop and gym is an Ortoo, and you'll find them on the map and in the “Around here” list.

The name comes from the relay stations of the Mongol Empire, where messengers rested and changed horses. An Ortoo is still a place to stop on your way.

On a day you order at an Ortoo, you get one part. Eight parts make one Ger. There are no points and no leaderboard: the Gers you have standing are your score.

02 — Getting started

Getting started takes a few minutes.

  1. Get the app. It's on the App Store for iPhone and Google Play for Android.
  2. Sign in with your phone number. Enter your number and tap “Send code”. Type the 6-digit code from the SMS and tap “Sign in”. No password, no email. For a number from another country, tap the flag inside the field and pick it.
  3. Pick your Nuudelchin. The app asks “Who are you?”, which picks the Nuudelchin who stands beside you. You can change it later in your Profile.
  4. Choose a name. It's optional, but it helps: staff use it to find your order when they approve it at the counter.

A gift

Your first eight parts are already here. Every new player starts with one of each, so your first Ger can go up at the first Ortoo where your order is approved.

03 — The app

Four tabs and one QR button.

Along the bottom are four tabs, with a round QR button beside them. The top bar shows your name and how many Gers you have standing.

Ortoo

The map, with a list of Ortoos you pull up over it.

  • “Around here” sorts nearest first. “For you” sorts by a Ger of yours about to fall, your missing parts and the walk.
  • “Find a part”: tap a part and only the Ortoos giving it today stay.
  • Filters: kind of place, opening hours, distance, “My Gers”, “About to fall”.
  • “Fresh stories”: from people who visited an Ortoo in the last 24 hours.
  • “Featured”: Ortoos that paid for their place. They sit at the top, under their own heading.

An Ortoo's page

Opens from a pin on the map, the list or a QR code. Today's and tomorrow's part, the menu, your basket, the “Raise a Ger” button, the loyalty ladder and stories are all here. “Directions” shows you the way.

Orders

Your orders and payments. An unfinished payment, or an order waiting for staff, sits at the top. Tap it and the app checks again.

Parts

How many of each part you hold, whether you're “Ready to build”, and the Gers you have standing. A Ger close to falling shows how many hours it has left.

Profile

Your name, your Nuudelchin, language and Ger count. Sign out and account deletion are here too. If you work at an Ortoo, the “Business” row is where you approve waiting orders.

QR button

Scan the Ortoo's QR code on the table to open its page straight away.

Map tools

Traffic, junction cameras, weather and directions. They don't touch the game, they just help you get around the city.

04 — Parts

Eight parts of a Ger, one of each.

A Ger takes one of each part. None is rarer than another.

  1. Toono

    The round window at the crown. Smoke goes out, sunlight comes in.

  2. Bagana

    The two posts that hold up the toono. By custom, nobody walks between them.

  3. Khaalga

    The wooden door that welcomes guests, always facing south.

  4. Orkh

    The felt flap over the toono, opened each morning and drawn closed at night.

  5. Esgii

    Sheep's-wool felt over the roof and walls that keeps the Ger warm.

  6. Busluur

    Three ropes around the outside that hold the Ger snug.

  7. Khana

    The folding lattice walls. A Ger's size is counted in khana.

  8. Uni

    Long roof poles from khana to toono. The only part lost if a Ger falls.

05 — Collecting

One part a day at every Ortoo.

Each Ortoo has its own part of the day, and its page shows it before you order: “Today here: Toono · Tomorrow: Bagana”.

Every Ortoo gives all eight parts in turn over eight days. Eight days in a row at one place make a full set. Each Ortoo is at a different point in the turn, so a few different places get you there sooner.

What counts as a visit

Your first approved order of the day at an Ortoo is your visit. An order is approved in one of two ways.

  • You pay in the app. At an Ortoo that takes payment in the app, fill your basket and tap “Pay”. As soon as the payment goes through, the part is yours.
  • Staff approve it. Tap “Register order” (or “Pay at the counter” where the Ortoo takes payment in the app) and pay at the counter in cash, by card or by transfer. Once staff approve it, the part is yours. Until then it reads “Waiting for staff to approve it”. On Android you get a notification when it's approved.

Good to remember

  • One part a day from each Ortoo. One coffee or ten, it's the same.
  • Another order there the same day is simply an order. It gives no part.
  • If your order is approved the next morning, you still get the part of the day you ordered.
  • Up to 10 orders can wait for approval at one Ortoo.
  • A payment started in the app is open for 15 minutes. If your money moved after that, the part still arrives.
  • The day turns over at midnight, Ulaanbaatar time.

Planning ahead

The turn is always the same: Toono → Bagana → Khaalga → Orkh → Esgii → Busluur → Khana → Uni, then Toono again. Once you know an Ortoo's part today, you can work out its week ahead.

To track down a missing part, use “Find a part” in the list: a part you lack says “Missing”, the others say how many places give them today.

06 — Raising a Ger

With all eight, raise your Ger.

Once you hold one of each part, the page of an Ortoo where you've ordered says “Ready to build”. Tap “Raise a Ger”: the eight parts are used, and your Ger goes up at that Ortoo.

  • Raise it where you order. It takes an approved order at that Ortoo within its time, the same time a Ger there stands for. Not necessarily today's. With the parts but no order there, the page says “Ready: order here to raise your Ger”.
  • Pick somewhere you go often. A Ger stands while you keep going back.
  • One Ger a day at each Ortoo. You can hold as many as you like, at one place or at many.
  • It tells you what's missing. The page says “Still needed for a Ger: …”.

“Your Ger is up!”

07 — Keeping a Ger

Keep going back and your Ger keeps standing.

Your Ger stands for as long as you keep having approved orders at the Ortoo where you raised it. Each Ger counts only its own Ortoo, so visiting other places every day doesn't change it.

How long it stands

Each Ortoo sets its own time: at least 7 days, longer at some, and at some a Ger never falls. The clock starts when you raise it, and starts again with every approved order there. An order still waiting for approval doesn't count.

You'll hear in good time

Two days before it falls, the app marks your Ger “About to fall”, and the Parts tab shows how many hours it has left. On Android you also get a notification. One approved order at that Ortoo starts the clock again.

If it falls

You keep seven of the eight parts. Only the Uni is lost, which is also what breaks first on a real Ger. Find one Uni and you can raise it again.

Nobody can touch another player's Ger. A Ger only falls after a long gap.

08 — What Gers are for

The Gers you have standing are your score.

  • Your score. There are no points, levels or leaderboards. Your Profile shows your total. The map, the list and each Ortoo's page show how many of yours stand there, as in “You have 3 Gers here”. At every Ortoo you only see your own.
  • The Ortoo's rewards. Some Ortoos set a loyalty ladder by how many of your Gers stand there, for example “3 Gers — a free coffee”. You'll find the ladder on the Ortoo's page. The place honours it at its own counter; the app only counts.

09 — Stories

Tell others about places you've been.

Once an order of yours is approved at an Ortoo, you can post a story there. Answer “How was it?” with one of four: Disliked, Okay, Liked or Favorite, and add a few words.

  • Up to 1,000 characters, and up to 5 photos and videos in all; one can be a video of up to 15 seconds.
  • One story per Ortoo. Post again and it replaces the last one.
  • A story with a photo or video sits in the “Fresh stories” ring for 24 hours, then stays on the Ortoo's page.
  • You can share your story as a picture to Instagram and other apps, and delete it whenever you like.
  • An Ortoo can't hide or delete stories. A story gives no part and doesn't change where an Ortoo sits in the list.

Questions

Something on your mind? Let's answer it here.

Do I start from zero?

No. Every new player starts with all eight parts, so your first Ger can go up at the first Ortoo where your order is approved.

I paid, but no part came.

Open the Orders tab and tap the payment at the top, and the app checks again. This happens when you pay in your bank app and don't come back to Nutag. If your money moved, the part arrives even if it's late.

It says “You have already collected here today”.

Each Ortoo gives one part a day. Your order went through as normal, it just gives no part. Come back tomorrow, or try another Ortoo.

My order isn't being approved.

Tell the staff at the counter your name, and they'll find your order by it. If they reject it, the order is removed and you can register it again. An order still waiting gives no part and doesn't keep a Ger standing.

Do I get more parts if I order more?

No. One coffee or ten, each Ortoo gives one part a day, so just order what you'd like.

Is the part I get from an order random?

No. Today's and tomorrow's part are shown before you order.

The “Raise a Ger” button won't work.

Either a part is missing (the page says which), you have no approved order at this Ortoo within its time (the page says to order first), or you've already raised a Ger here today. You can raise another tomorrow, or at a different Ortoo.

Do I have to be there to raise a Ger?

Only to order. You need an approved order at that Ortoo within its time; the tap itself works from anywhere. A Ger stands while you keep going back, so raise it somewhere you go often.

What if I can't go back for a while?

Each place sets how long: at least 7 days, longer at some, and at some a Ger never falls. The app tells you two days before. Even if it falls you keep 7 of the 8 parts, so you're only one Uni short.

Can someone knock my Ger down?

No. Nobody can touch another player's Ger.

Do notifications come on iPhone?

Not yet, they only come on Android for now. On iPhone, the Parts tab, the top bar and the map show when a Ger is close to falling.

Can I play without sharing my location?

Yes. The map and the list still work. Sorting by “Around here”, the distance filter and directions need your location. Ordering, scanning a QR code and raising a Ger don't.

There's a QR or NFC card on my table. What is it?

Some Ortoos put these on their tables. Scan it with the QR button or your phone's camera, or hold your phone to the card, and that Ortoo opens at your table. Without the app, an ordering page opens on nutag.fun, where you sign in with your phone number and order. Sign in to the app later with the same number and your part will be waiting.

I work at an Ortoo. Where do I approve orders?

Open the “Business” row in your Profile and you'll find “Waiting orders”. Approve them there and record whether it was cash, card or transfer. You can also approve them in the console at console.nutag.fun.

How do I delete my account?

Profile → “Delete account”. Your account and everything tied to it is permanently deleted within 30 days.

For business

Want your place to be an Ortoo? The storefront is free.

Your name, logo, banner, colours, intro, opening hours, menu, photo library, loyalty ladder and how long a Ger stands are all yours to set. Nutag never takes a cut of your sales. Two things cost money: a “Featured” spot in the list (39,000₮ a month) and table cards (12,000₮ each).

If you like, put QR or NFC cards on your tables, so customers can order from their seat even without the app. Fill in a short form and we'll give you a call.

How to play

Updated 2026-09-06

Nutag is a collecting game played at real places. Every time you order at a partner café, shop or gym (an Ortoo) you get one part of a Ger. Collect all eight and you raise a Ger at that Ortoo. Keep it standing by coming back every week.

In one line: go to an Ortoo → order → take today's part → with all eight, tap "Raise a ger" → come back at least once every 7 days to keep it.

1. Getting started

  1. Sign in with your phone number. Enter the 6-digit code sent by SMS. No password, no email.
  2. Pick your Nuudelchin, the nomad who stands beside you. You can change it later in your profile.
  3. Choose a name (optional). Staff use it to find your order when they approve it at the counter, so a name helps.

Every new player starts with one of each of the eight parts. That means your very first order at any Ortoo can already be your first Ger.

3. The eight parts of a Ger

A Mongolian ger is made of eight main parts. The Parts tab shows them in this same order. A Ger takes one of each.

Toono

The crown: the round wooden ring at the very top. Smoke leaves and light enters through it, and every roof pole rests on it. The most treasured part of the ger. The Ortoo pins on the map are drawn as a toono.

Bagana

The two upright pillars that hold the toono from below and carry the weight of the roof.

Khaalga

The wooden door, always facing south. The only way in.

Orkh

The square felt flap that covers the toono. Opened in the morning for light and air, closed against rain and at night.

Esgii

Felt pressed from sheep's wool. It covers the walls and the roof and keeps the ger warm. In the game, roof felt and wall felt are one part.

Busluur

The ropes belted around the outside of the ger. They hold the walls and the felt tight against the wind.

Khana

The folding lattice wall sections, woven from willow. They form the circle of the ger and carry the roof poles.

Uni

The long thin roof poles running from the top of the walls to the toono, dozens of them in a real ger. The most fragile part: when a Ger falls, only the uni is lost. Every other part comes back to you.

4. How to collect parts

Every Ortoo gives one part a day. If it gives a toono today, it gives a bagana tomorrow, then a khaalga, and so on through all eight over eight days. The Ortoo's page says so: "Today here: Toono · Tomorrow: Bagana". So eight visits to one Ortoo make a whole Ger. To collect faster, visit several Ortoos in a day.

  1. Find an Ortoo. On the map or in the list. The list's "Find a part" row shows eight circles: "Missing" over a part you still need, and how many places give it today over the rest. Tap a circle and both the map and the list narrow to the Ortoos giving that part today.
  2. Go there and open its page. Scan the QR code on the table with the centre button, or tap the pin on the map.
  3. Order. Add items from the menu to your basket. Then one of two things:
    • Pay, at Ortoos that take payment in the app. Pick your bank app and pay (or pay by QR from another device). The part lands the moment the payment settles.
    • Register order, at Ortoos that do not take payment in the app. Order and pay at the counter as usual; staff find your name in their console and approve it. Until then the app shows "Waiting for staff to approve it".
  4. Take your part. The badge appears on screen and the count on the Parts tab goes up.

Rules

  • Once a day per Ortoo. A second order the same day is just a purchase; it gives no part.
  • One visit is one part, however many items you buy. Spending more gives no more.
  • A part is credited only after the payment settles, or after staff approve the order.
  • If approval comes late you still get the part of the day you ordered.
  • The game day turns at midnight Ulaanbaatar time (UTC+8).

Drops (optional)

Each day the map places three drops within 150 to 500 metres of you. Each pin shows which part it holds. Collecting a drop takes a one-off purchase through the App Store or Google Play. A drop is a paid way to skip one day's walk; the whole game is playable without them.

5. What a Ger is and what it is for

The Ger is your score. Nutag has no points and no levels: the number of Gers you have standing is your standing. It shows on your profile and on the Parts tab.

Building one

  • You need one of each of the eight parts. The Parts tab says "Ready to build"; if not, the Ortoo's page tells you what is still missing.
  • You must have ordered at that Ortoo today. Parts travel with you, but a Ger is built only where you actually are.
  • Tap "Raise a ger" on the Ortoo's page. The eight parts are spent and the Ger goes up there.
  • One Ger per Ortoo per day. Across different Ortoos and different days, as many as you like.

What a Ger gives you

  • Ground. The pin of an Ortoo where your Ger stands is filled in on the map. Its page reads "N gers stand here, X of them are yours". Many players' Gers can stand at one Ortoo side by side.
  • Vendor rewards. A vendor can set a ladder of rewards by Ger count at their Ortoo, for example "3 gers: a free coffee". The ladder is on the Ortoo's page and your Gers there are measured against it. The vendor hands the reward over at the counter.
  • Better suggestions. The "For you" view ranks Ortoos by your Gers, your missing parts and the walk. A Ger about to fall always comes first.

Keeping it, and falling

  • If you do not order at that Ortoo for 7 days, your Ger there falls. Nobody can knock it down; only staying away does.
  • The clock is per Ortoo. Visiting a different café every day does not save the Ger at the first one.
  • The app warns you first: your Nuudelchin says "Your ger is about to fall!", the Ger is marked "About to fall" on your profile, and its pin changes on the map. One order at that Ortoo resets the clock.
  • When a Ger falls, seven parts come back and the uni is lost. Rebuilding costs one uni and one visit.

7. Common questions

I paid but got no part. Open the Orders tab and tap the unpaid checkout at the top; the app checks the payment again. This is how you get your part if you paid in your bank app and closed Nutag. A checkout is cancelled after 15 minutes, but if the money moves later the part is still credited.

"You have already collected here today." One part per Ortoo per day. Come back tomorrow, or visit another Ortoo.

"Raise a ger" is greyed out. Either a part is missing, or you have not ordered at this Ortoo today. The page says which.

My Ger fell. Seven parts are back on your Parts tab. Collect one uni, then order at the Ortoo where you want to rebuild.

What if I refuse location access? The map and the list still work; only nearest sorting, the distance filter and drops stop. Scanning, ordering and building never need it.
